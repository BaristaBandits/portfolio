Worked as a Powertrain Engineer in Raftar Formula Racing, IIT Madras’ Formula Student electric vehicle team.

## Website

[Raftar Formula Racing](https://www.raftariitm.com/)

## Responsibilities

- Designed and tested EV circuitry for the low-voltage subsystem
- Worked on system integration and validation for race conditions
- Collaborated with cross-functional teams in vehicle electronics

## Highlights

- Team secured Runner-up position at Formula Bharat 2024
- Contributed to building an international-level electric race car
- Gained hands-on experience in EV systems and embedded hardware
