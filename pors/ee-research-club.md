# Core Member – EE Research Club

Part of the EE Research Club at IIT Madras, helping cultivate a strong undergraduate research culture through events, discussions, and outreach activities.

## Website

[EE Research Club IIT Madras](https://sites.google.com/smail.iitm.ac.in/eerc/home)

## Responsibilities

- Organized research socials, technical talks, and professor interviews
- Coordinated events for a 30-member research-focused student community
- Encouraged undergraduate participation in research activities
- Helped students connect with faculty and ongoing research projects

## Highlights

- Built an active interdisciplinary research community
- Conducted events focused on scientific communication and exploration
- Facilitated interactions between students and researchers
