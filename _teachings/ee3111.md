Teaching Assistant for the probability and statistics course for electrical engineering students covering:

- Random variables
- Estimation
- Hypothesis testing
- Statistical inference

## Syllabus

### Module 1: Probability Review & Sampling

- Random variables, Expectation, Covariance, Correlation
- Limit Theorems: Law of Large Numbers (LLN), Central Limit Theorem (CLT)
- Sampling Distributions: Sample Mean, Sample Variance
- Derived Distributions: Chi-Square (\(\chi^2\)), Student’s t, and F distributions

### Module 2: Theory of Estimation

- Point Estimation: Method of Moments (MOM), Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE)
- Bayesian Estimation: Priors, Posteriors, Conjugate Priors, MAP Estimators
- Evaluating Estimators: Bias-Variance Tradeoff, Risk, Cramér-Rao Lower Bound

### Module 3: Hypothesis Testing

- Fundamentals: Null/Alternative Hypotheses, Type I/II Errors, Power, p-values
- Parametric Tests: t-tests (One/Two sample), F-test for variances
- General Tests: Likelihood Ratio Tests (LRT), Chi-Square Goodness of Fit

### Module 4: Linear Regression

- Simple Linear Regression: Least Squares (OLS), Geometric Interpretation
- Inference: Hypothesis testing for slope, Confidence & Prediction Intervals
- Diagnostics: Residual analysis, \(R^2\), Model assumptions

### Module 5: Statistical Learning

- Learning Theory: Minimax Risk, Sample Complexity, Overfitting
- High-Dimensional Statistics: Regularization (Ridge/Lasso), Curse of Dimensionality
- Classification: Logistic Regression, ROC Curves, AUC
