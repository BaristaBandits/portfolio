## Teaching

I have served as a Teaching Assistant for undergraduate courses in electrical engineering and computing at IIT Madras and Stanford.

My responsibilities included:
- Conducting tutorial sessions
- Assisting students with assignments and labs
- Preparing course material and evaluations
- Holding office hours and doubt-clearing sessions

I particularly enjoy teaching topics at the intersection of systems, computation, probability, and machine learning.
